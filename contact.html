Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries

How far in advance should I book my appointment?

For bridal makeup, we recommend booking 6-12 months in advance, especially during peak wedding season (May-October). For other special events, 4-6 weeks notice is ideal, though we can sometimes accommodate last-minute bookings depending on availability.

Do you travel to clients' locations?

Yes! We offer on-location services for weddings and special events. Travel fees may apply depending on the distance. For locations beyond 30 miles from our studio, additional charges will be discussed during consultation.

What happens during a makeup trial?

A makeup trial typically lasts 1.5-2 hours. We'll discuss your vision, preferences, and any reference photos you bring. We'll create your desired look and make adjustments until you're completely satisfied. We recommend scheduling your trial 1-3 months before your event.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, Venmo, and cash. For weddings and large group bookings, a 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your date, with the remaining balance due 14 days before the event.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling of regular appointments. For bridal and event bookings, cancellations must be made at least 30 days in advance for a partial refund (excluding the non-refundable deposit).
