Our Founder

The Vision Behind Blush and Bloom

Sophia Reynolds

Founder & Lead Makeup Artist

With over a decade of experience in the beauty industry, Sophia Reynolds founded Blush and Bloom with a vision to create personalized makeup experiences that enhance each client's natural beauty.

After graduating from the prestigious London Academy of Beauty Arts and working with celebrity clients across Europe, Sophia returned home with a dream to bring high-end makeup artistry to everyday clients for their special moments.

"I believe that makeup should never mask who you are, but rather unveil your most radiant self. Every face tells a unique story, and my passion is highlighting that individuality through the art of makeup."

Sophia's work has been featured in numerous bridal magazines, and her expertise in creating long-lasting, photogenic looks has made her a sought-after artist for weddings and special events.

Our Philosophy

The Blush and Bloom Approach

At Blush and Bloom, we believe that makeup is an art form that should enhance your natural beauty, not mask it. Our approach is centered around these core principles:

Personalization

We recognize that every face is unique, with its own features, skin tone, and texture. Our approach begins with understanding your individual characteristics and preferences, creating a look that's tailored specifically to you.

Natural Enhancement

We focus on enhancing your natural features rather than changing them. Our techniques are designed to bring out your best qualities while maintaining a look that feels authentically you.

Photogenic Finishes

Understanding how makeup translates in photographs is essential, especially for special events. We use techniques and products that ensure your makeup looks beautiful both in person and in photos.

Longevity

We prioritize products and application techniques that create long-lasting looks, ensuring you remain flawless throughout your entire event without constant touch-ups.

"Makeup should never be about following trends blindly, but about discovering what makes you feel most confident and radiant."

— Sophia Reynolds, Founder
