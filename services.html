Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How far in advance should I book my bridal makeup?

We recommend booking your bridal makeup at least 6 months in advance, especially for peak wedding season (May-October). This ensures you have time for your consultation and trial run before the big day.

Do you travel to wedding venues?

Yes! We offer on-location services for weddings and special events. Travel fees may apply depending on the distance. Please inquire for specific details.

What brands do you use?

We use a curated selection of professional-grade products from brands like Charlotte Tilbury, Pat McGrath, NARS, Armani Beauty, and Hourglass. We also customize our kit based on your skin type and preferences.

How long does a makeup application take?

A standard makeup application takes approximately 45-60 minutes. Bridal makeup may take 60-90 minutes to ensure every detail is perfect.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee. For bridal bookings, a non-refundable deposit is required to secure your date.

How do the membership plans work?

Our membership plans are billed monthly and include a set number of makeup applications per month/quarter. Members also receive exclusive discounts, priority booking, and special perks. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice.
